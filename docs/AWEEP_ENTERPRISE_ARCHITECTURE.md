# AWEEP — Enterprise Architecture Guide

## Overview
The **AI Workforce Enterprise Extension Pack (AWEEP)** extends the platform with 11 strategic enterprise capabilities:

1. **Multi-Client Portal**: Manage multiple client organizations from a single partner account without data leakage.
2. **No-Code Workflow Builder**: Visual canvas, DAG validation, versioning, human approval gates.
3. **AI Team & Department Orchestrator**: Multi-agent team topologies (Pipeline, Star, Parallel, Hierarchical).
4. **Enterprise Search**: Cross-system RAG search with strict security clearance and document citations.
5. **Continuous Improvement Lab**: Prompt A/B testing and statistical evaluation of performance improvements.
6. **Compliance Evidence Vault**: Immutable audit trail with SHA-256 digital signatures.
7. **Secure Computer-Use / RPA Gateway**: Controlled UI automation in isolated sandboxes.
8. **Connector Marketplace**: Verified ERP, CRM, and Banking connectors.
9. **Industry & Jurisdiction Marketplace**: Angola, Portugal, Brazil, and Mozambican tax/regulatory packs.
10. **Data Residency Layer**: Strict region enforcement (Luanda, Frankfurt, US East) and compliance fencing.
11. **Identity Lifecycle (SCIM)**: Automatic provisioning, deprivisioning, and department transfers.
